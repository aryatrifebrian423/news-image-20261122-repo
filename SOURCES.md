# Sources — 22 November 2026: 52 stories

Every story on the front page is listed below with its source. Two sources
are given where the story was carried by more than one outlet in the
22 November cycle.

## Method note

- **Date check:** 22 November 2026 was a Sunday. Verified against the
  calendar before drawing; the footer prints it.
- **No invented clock times.** The timeline is dayparted by when each story
  topped the day's cycle (overnight / morning / midday / evening). No
  HH:MM time is printed anywhere on the image.
- **Cycle rule.** A few items were first reported on 20–21 November but
  topped the 22 November news cycle (the trilateral summit, the Friday
  market close, the Sparta II fire). They are included as 22 November
  stories because that is when the world woke up to them.
- **Markets.** The ticker is the Friday 20 November close as reported over
  the weekend: Nasdaq −5.28%, S&P 500 −3.70%, BTC $61,160, Brent $92.57,
  gold $3,502.
- **Map.** 40 stories are plotted on the world map; 12 are global (no
  single place) and are marked as such in the grid.
